# BRIEF: archive-live

Interviewed 2026-09-09. Answers are the human's selections, verbatim.

## The eight interview answers

1. **Vibe + references:** "Operator console, live, exact." References offered and accepted: a NORAD floor, a Bloomberg terminal, the tape decks in a recording studio.
2. **Scroll journey:** Identity → Builds → Profile → Stack → Contact. "Show the work before the story. Vantage/Raphel/Daysi hit within the first two screens; the bio comes after the visitor already believes you ship."
3. **Energy curve:** "Calm open, build to Builds, calm close." Quiet boot, the surface fills as you scroll, Builds is the loudest, then it strips back to a single cursor.
4. **Feeling, stage by stage, and the ONE moment:** curiosity (boot) → confidence (builds expand with real links, real collaborators) → recognition (the bio names the path) → competence (stack) → readiness (cursor waiting). The moment: "the builds list opens like a file."
5. **One thing no site does:** "The photo develops as you scroll."
6. **Aesthetic range:** Premium-minimal, sharpened. Same dark canvas and single signal green; the premium lives in craft, not colour.
7. **One world or distinct scenes:** Live surface. One scrolling page that behaves like a running terminal/archive tool; the sidebar is real app chrome; the close is an actual input.
8. **Assets owned:** one full-body photo (to be cropped waist-up 3:4), the September 2026 resume PDF. No footage, no brand kit. Nothing generated.

Additional decisions from the same interview:

- Audience: hiring managers AND clients/founders. One action: email. Label everywhere: **Open a line**.
- Keep: ASCII name banner, ASCII cat, binary background texture. Cut: NYC clock.
- Builds list: click, one open at a time, keyboard navigable. Rows: Vantage, Raphel, Daysi Collection, Sentry, Twenty CRM i18n. Dropped: MatchPoint, MHS2, Anchore, Job Tracker.
- Bio: first person. Title line: "AI Engineer · Full-Stack Developer".
- Deploy: Netlify at root, no custom domain yet, GitHub Pages removed.

## Grammar

**Live surface** (uniqueness.md §2.3). Bans in force: no `scrub`, no `kinetic`, no `spotlight`, no `magnet`, `drift` at most two stops, no display headings, no marketing chrome, no hero claim over media. Every panel is real markup computing real state.

Why the other seven lost: filmic one-shot needs footage and a carried argument, and it is the burdened default; chaptered editorial reads as "read" not "operated"; continuous world needs a filmed or generated place; typographic poster forbids the photo and the list; gallery/catalog fits the builds section alone; split stage has no two-sided argument; rhythmic cutlist is the wrong pulse for a calm open and close.

## The feeling curve

```
1  Curiosity    the console is already on: banner and cat at p=0, three status lines land in sequence, the portrait is still characters   (pin, 2.0)
2  Confidence   five rectangular rows enter in order; opening one unfolds a real dossier with live link, collaborators, stack; the portrait completes on the first open   (flow + in, accordion, count; PEAK, tallest on the page)
3  Recognition  first-person lines cross over inside a held frame, each one connecting to the last   (pin, 2.4)
4  Competence   four labelled groups travel laterally, typeset as museum labels   (pan, 1.8)
5  Readiness    the page stops on a prompt with a cursor in it; ground present from p=0, cue holds   (pin, closing, 1.4)
```

Total about 10.6 viewport-heights across 5 acts. No device twice in a row. Silence before the peak is the calm of act 1.
